# Visual direction

The owner's target look for the screen. Each step below is one milestone in [`TODO.md`](TODO.md).

## The target

- The farm is the hero: big and centred, with the whole screen behind it.
- The script is a floating window over the farm. It can be moved, resized, closed and brought back, and it has its own Run, Step and Stop.
- Items show as icons with counts in a bar at the top left.
- A few round buttons at the top right open the Unlocks, Help and Menu as overlays, instead of a fixed side column.
- The field is a 3D low-poly field, seen from an angle, with growing crops and a drone that hovers over it.
- Harvests give feedback: smooth drone movement, particles, "+N" popups and light.

## Rules

- Original art only: everything is drawn from Godot primitives and the default theme.
- No copied assets, icons, layouts or names from other games.
